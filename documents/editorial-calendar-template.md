## Purpose

A template structure for organising the editorial calendar output. Use this format to track content from planning through publication.

### Status Definitions

- **Planned** — topic approved, not yet briefed
- **Briefed** — content brief completed, assigned to writer
- **In Progress** — writer is working on the draft
- **Review** — draft submitted for editorial review
- **Final** — editorial review complete, ready for publishing
- **Published** — live and distributed
- **On Hold** — paused due to dependencies or resource constraints

## Usage Notes

- Update status weekly at minimum
- Review and adjust the calendar at the start of each month
- Track actual publication dates against planned dates to improve future scheduling
- Archive completed months for year-over-year comparison
